Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Trix & Tarneeb Scores mobile app (package name com.kareemdaker.trixscore on Google Play, also available on the Apple App Store), developed and published by Kareem Daker ("we", "us").

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name or contact details. The games and scores you enter are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

The app uses third-party services that automatically collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and app usage and crash data:

  • Google AdMob and its ad partners, to show ads, which may be personalized where you have agreed to it,
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), to understand app usage and fix crashes.

These providers process this information under their own privacy policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, which we use only to reply.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the service providers above, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We do not keep our own copy of the information collected by these services. They retain it for a limited time under their own policies. Uninstalling the app deletes all game data on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can reset or limit use of your advertising ID in your device settings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change it any time from Ad Privacy in the app's Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete your information or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Security

Information is sent to these providers over encrypted connections.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.
