Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This privacy policy is for the Trix & Tarneeb Scores mobile app (formerly Trix Scores), available on Google Play under the package name com.kareemdaker.trixscore and on the Apple App Store. The app is developed and published by Kareem Daker, an individual developer ("we", "us" or "our" in this policy).

It explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at support@trixscores.com.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account to use the app, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The games, player names and scores you enter are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • The app shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting from Google. These services collect device and usage information as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The games you create, the player and team names you type, the scores of each round, and your app settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive or have access to this information. Deleting a game removes it, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If your device's own backup service is turned on (such as Android backup or iCloud backup), your device may include the app's data in that backup, under your Google or Apple account.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app includes the following services, which collect information automatically when you use the app. Each provider handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may also request ads from Meta Audience Network through mediation. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS where you allow it),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and network type,
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

Ads may be personalized based on this information where the law allows it and you have agreed to it. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time from Ad Privacy in the app's Settings.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened, which game types are played, and whether ads load. It collects an app instance identifier, device information, the app version, your approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address), and events about how the app is used, along with a few app-level details such as the number of games saved. It does not collect your name, contacts, or the names and scores you enter.

Crash reporting: Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report so we can fix it. A report includes a Crashlytics installation identifier, the device model, operating system and app version, and technical details of the error and the state of the app when it happened.

App configuration: Firebase Remote Config

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to receive settings from us without an app update. It uses an app instance identifier to fetch those settings.

You can read how these providers handle information here:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Meta Privacy Policy

4. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, including through the feedback options in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. Emails sent from the app also include your app version, device model and operating system version, to help us look into problems. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the app, and we do not share it.

5. How we use information

  • to show ads, which keep the app free,
  • to understand how the app is used and decide what to improve,
  • to find and fix crashes and bugs,
  • to respond to your questions and feedback.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except:

  • with the service providers named in section 3, which collect it directly through the app,
  • when required by law, such as in response to a court order,
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.

7. Retention

We do not keep our own copy of the information described in section 3. Analytics data and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited time according to Firebase's retention settings and then deleted. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to answer them.

8. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under your device's privacy or Google ad settings. On iOS, you can choose whether apps may track you under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also use Ad Privacy in the app's Settings.
  • Your game data: delete individual games in the app, or uninstall the app to delete all of it.
  • Other requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. The app does not identify you to us, but we will help with any request we can. Email support@trixscores.com.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Information collected by the services in section 3 is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and keep the information we handle to a minimum.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
